kiddoh

Little moments, close to home.

Kiddoh helps parents of children from birth to age 4 find things to do: story times, classes, and playgrounds nearby, simple play ideas for home that fit your space, and a community of parents whose kids are the same age as yours.

  • Made for ages 0 to 4
  • Starting in Boston and nearby towns: Cambridge, Somerville, Brookline, Newton, Watertown, Milton, Quincy, Dedham, and Norwood
  • Free to use

Kiddoh is in a small private beta with Boston-area parents. If you'd like to join, email us.

Your family's privacy

Kiddoh asks only for what it needs, never sells your information, and has no ads. Read the privacy policy for the details.
